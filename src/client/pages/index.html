Questions we get asked

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. A simple business website costs far less than a booking system with customer accounts. After a short call we send a fixed quote, so the price does not change halfway through.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take two to four weeks. Booking systems and custom applications usually take four to ten weeks, depending on scope. You get a timeline with your quote.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Where it makes sense we build an admin page so you can edit services, prices, projects and see enquiries without touching code.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes. We set up hosting on Cloudflare, which is fast in South Africa and often free for small sites, and register the domain in your name.

Do you work with businesses outside Limpopo?

Yes. We are based in Limpopo and work with clients across South Africa over calls, WhatsApp and email.

What happens after launch?

We fix any bugs for free for 30 days. After that you can choose a monthly care plan or contact us whenever you need changes.

Why I started Neuropulse

See what we have built

I am a Computer Science Diploma graduate from Tshwane University of Technology, with a strong interest in backend engineering, databases and turning ideas into software people actually use. I created Neuropulse Inc. during my studies as an enterprise initiative, with TaskFlow, a task-management application, as our first product.

Before writing code professionally I worked in order taking at McDonald's Mokopane. That fast-paced, customer-facing job taught me something I still build around today: people want things to be quick, clear and correct the first time.

Today I am a software development intern at TUT Polokwane through ICEP, where I work on real systems such as the Limpopo Varsity Hackathon application portal and the Lecture Room Booking Management System. Everything I learn there goes straight into the work we do for Neuropulse clients.

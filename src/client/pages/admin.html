      Two-factor sign-in

      Checking…

      Adds a 6-digit code from your phone to every sign-in, so a stolen password alone cannot open this dashboard. Works with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy and 1Password.

      1. Open your authenticator app and choose Add account, then Enter a setup key.
      2. Account name: Neuropulse Admin. Key (type of key: time-based):
      3. Type the 6-digit code the app now shows:

      Keep your phone safe: if you lose the app you will need to remove the setup key from the database to sign in again.

      Two-factor sign-in is on for your account. To turn it off, enter your password and a current code.

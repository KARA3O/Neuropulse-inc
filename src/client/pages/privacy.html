Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. This policy explains what personal information Neuropulse Inc. collects through this website and how we protect it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you send a project enquiry we collect your name, email address, and, if you give them, your phone number, business name and project details. When you subscribe to updates we collect your email address.

To stop spam and abuse we keep a one-way, salted fingerprint of your IP address. We cannot turn it back into your IP address.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and, if you subscribed, to tell you about new work. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare may process data outside South Africa under appropriate safeguards. Access to enquiries is limited to Neuropulse staff with a password-protected account.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 24 months, or longer if you become a client and we need records for the project. Subscribers can leave at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email {{site.email}} and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Cookies

The public website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The admin area uses one essential cookie to keep staff signed in.
